A clear licensing boundary

The existing MIT core stays MIT. The CSS framework, published themes, components and free examples remain open for commercial use. Any future commercial license would apply only to separately authored premium template and asset files. It cannot revoke MIT rights in the open-source files.

Premium license terms are still being reviewed. One-time purchase concepts would grant perpetual use of purchased versions, with any update period specified at launch. A future subscription would require continuing value. None of those terms is offered today.

Questions before a paid launch

Can I use the free core for commercial projects?

Yes. Syntyr Core is MIT-licensed, including commercial use. Review the license and published release boundaries.

Can I purchase Pro, Studio or Console Kit now?

No. They are research concepts or private prototypes. There is no payment, preorder, customer download or guaranteed launch date.

Is the free Studio color theme part of the paid Studio tier?

No. The Studio theme is included in the MIT core. The proposed paid Studio tier describes additional, separately authored website and application templates; no commercial bundle is available yet.

Will Syntyr require React, Tailwind or hosted services?

No required framework or service is planned. Proposed assets would use native HTML and CSS and work with supported backend or frontend stacks. Complex application behavior remains the responsibility of your code.

Can I inspect the work before expressing interest?

Yes. The Console Kit research page contains genuine static screenshots with synthetic data; free public examples demonstrate the existing MIT library.
