How a project is scoped

  1. Describe the real use case. Share target users, stack, pages, constraints and rough timeline, without sending credentials or confidential customer records.
  2. Review a concrete proposal. Scope, deliverables, dependencies, acceptance criteria, change requests, pricing, ownership and support period are agreed in writing before work begins.
  3. Validate and hand off. Review the output against the agreed acceptance checklist and receive the source files and integration notes specified in the contract.
