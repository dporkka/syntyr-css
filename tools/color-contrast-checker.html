    How to use this tool

    WCAG 2.2 success criterion 1.4.3 requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for qualifying large text at AA. AAA uses 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text. The text-size definitions are specific; simply making a heading bold does not automatically qualify.

    Contrast alone cannot certify that a component or website is accessible. Check your actual typography, focus states and interactive controls. For broader guidance, read Syntyr's accessibility notes.

    Source: WCAG 2.2 Contrast (Minimum) and Contrast (Enhanced).
