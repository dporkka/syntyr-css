Fieldwork: a clearer work queue.

A conceptual redesign exploring how a small team might triage field requests. All details are illustrative, not client claims.

FW / 01

Context

Teams need to know which requests are ready, who owns them, and what evidence remains missing. The concept separates those questions from decorative status.

Decisions

  • Prioritize scan-friendly labels.
  • Use native form controls instead of custom interaction primitives.
  • Let lower-priority information follow the primary task.

Outcome

This is a design illustration, not a measured production outcome. A real case study should include consented project artifacts and substantiated results.
