Designing a calmer interface.

A clear interface helps readers establish context, decide what matters, and leave when they are done.

Use a readable rhythm

Make line lengths comfortable, separate sections consistently, and let the heading hierarchy communicate structure.

Reduce unneeded movement

Reserve motion for immediate feedback rather than decoration. Native disclosure gives readers control over additional details.

Respect the user's choices

Preserve keyboard navigation, readable contrast and the ability to zoom. A design that works without scripts is a strong baseline.
