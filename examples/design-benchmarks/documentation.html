Accessibility checklist

  • Use descriptive landmarks and headings.
  • Keep links visibly distinguishable.
  • Retain focus and readable reflow at narrow widths.
  • Test zoom, forced colors, and assistive technology.

This accessible-looking sample is not an accessibility certification; no search, authentication or storage is implemented.
