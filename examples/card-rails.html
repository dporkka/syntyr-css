Implementation notes

Load syntyr.core.css and the opt-in syntyr.recipes.css. The row uses native horizontal overflow and proximity snap, with a visible scrollbar and a peek at the next card. Its width is controlled by --syn-card-rail-width; its gap by --syn-card-rail-gap. Reuse any card component inside the track.

This static gallery is not a playlist, filtering engine, autoplay system, or server-backed navigator. For full usage and accessibility guidance, see Composition recipes.
